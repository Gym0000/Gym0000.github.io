About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Cyberspace Security at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Honggang Hu. I received my B.Eng. in Information Security from USTC in 2023.

My research focuses on lattice algorithms and their applications to cryptanalysis, with a current emphasis on the Shortest Vector Problem (SVP), discrete Gaussian sampling, and Learning With Errors (LWE). I study how sampling, sieving, and decoding techniques can lead to faster algorithms with rigorous time and space bounds.

I also work on lattice-based methods in computational number theory, including integer factorization, RSA cryptanalysis, and small-root problems via Coppersmith's method. My broader interests lie in theoretical computer science, particularly subset sum and related problems.
